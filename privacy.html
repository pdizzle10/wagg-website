3. The app — two kinds of user, two roles under data protection law

Wagg is a B2B tool: our direct customers ("Owners") are self-employed pet service providers who use Wagg to run their own business. Owners' clients ("Pet Owners") and any staff Owners employ ("Team Members") also have data in the system, but they don't sign up with us directly — the Owner adds them.

This split matters legally:

  • Where we are the controller: your account details, subscription/billing data, and how you use the app (analytics). We decide why and how this data is processed, so UK GDPR obligations sit with us directly.
  • Where we are the processor: the personal data an Owner enters about their Pet Owners, their pets, and their Team Members. The Owner is the controller of that data — they decide what to collect and why, and are responsible for having a lawful basis to hold it (e.g. their own contract with their client). We process it under a Data Processing Addendum agreed as part of our Terms, which sets out our obligations as processor.
  • Financial and tax data submitted via MTD: you remain responsible for the accuracy of what's submitted; we're responsible for transmitting it securely to HMRC via their API and storing your submission history. HMRC is a joint controller of tax data you choose to submit, not our sub-processor.

If you're a Pet Owner or Team Member whose data appears in someone else's Wagg account and you have a query about it, the correct first point of contact is the Owner (your dog walker/sitter/groomer), since they're the controller. We'll assist them in responding to you, and won't refuse to engage directly if you contact us instead.

4. What we collect (the app)

Account data (Owner): name, email, phone, business name, address, password (hashed), profile photo.

Team Member data: name, email, phone, role, hourly rate/pay data if entered, which bookings they're assigned to. Team Members do not see pricing data belonging to the Owner's business (enforced technically, not just by policy).

Client & pet data (entered by the Owner): Pet Owner name, contact details, address; pet name, breed, medical/behavioural notes, vet contact details, feeding/care instructions — whatever the Owner chooses to record.

Scheduling data: bookings, routines, walk/visit session logs, GPS-derived route data if location tracking is used during a session.

Financial data: invoices, line items, expenses, payment records (marked-as-paid status; we do not currently process card payments on the Owner's behalf), and — for MTD users — quarterly submission data and Final Declaration data submitted to HMRC, plus HMRC OAuth tokens that authorise us to submit on your behalf.

Open banking data (future feature, not yet live): if/when enabled, read-only transaction data via an open banking provider (TrueLayer/Yapily/Tink/Plaid — provider TBC), used only to match payments against invoices. This will never include payment initiation — we cannot move money.

Subscription/billing data: handled by Stripe. We hold your subscription status, plan, and billing interval; full card details are never held by us — Stripe processes and stores them under PCI DSS.

Technical data: device type, OS version, crash reports (Sentry), in-app usage/analytics events (PostHog), IP address, app version.

6. Where your data is stored and who processes it

We use the following sub-processors. Where a business Owner's client/pet data flows through these, they act as sub-processors on our instructions, and by extension on the Owner's.

  • Google / Meta — website analytics and ad pixel, once switched on (see section 2).
  • [Open banking provider — TBC] — read-only transaction data, once live.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

International transfers: all current and planned infrastructure is EU-hosted. If any future sub-processor sits outside the UK/EEA, we'll rely on the UK's International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or an adequacy decision, and update this policy before making the change.

7. Retention
  • Financial records (invoices, expenses, payments, MTD submissions): retained for 7 years, matching HMRC's record-keeping requirement. Deletion of this data is technically restricted at the database level even if you close your account, until the 7-year period has passed.
  • Account, client, and pet data: retained while your account is active. On account closure, data is soft-deleted (marked inactive, no longer visible in-app) immediately, then permanently erased after [90 DAYS — CONFIRM] unless you request earlier erasure or we're required to keep it (e.g. financial records above).
  • Team Member data: on removal, personal identifiers (name, email, phone) are wiped; the record itself is kept (without personal data) so that historical bookings and audit/change logs remain intact.
  • Crash/analytics data: retained per Sentry/PostHog's standard windows — typically 90 days for Sentry, longer in aggregated/anonymised form for PostHog.
  • Google Form responses (website): kept until early-access decisions are made, then deleted or migrated into the app's systems.
8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data (subject to our legal retention obligations above)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Port your data to another provider in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To exercise any of these, email hello@wagg-app.com. We'll respond within one month. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

If you're a Pet Owner or Team Member (not the Owner/account holder), we'll generally direct rights requests to the relevant Owner first, since they're the controller of that data — but we'll still assist and won't refuse to engage.

9. Security
